# Lumen Repository Contract

## Role

Lumen is the executable evidence runtime in the Weaver Nexus three-repository master build.
It owns Chronicle, receipt, replay, persistent replay-cache behavior, witness packaging, bounded provider interfaces, and disabled-by-default donor adapters.

Final constitutional policy and promotion authority remain in `Weaver_Os`. Formal proof source and Auto Scientist experiments remain in `Math_Build1994`.

## Permanent laws

- No receipt, no promotion.
- Replay must reconstruct governance-relevant state, not merely reprint logs.
- Missing pins, licenses, receipts, or authority leases fail closed.
- Recommendation is not authorization.
- Runtime capability cannot self-promote evidence or authority.
- Simulation is not physical validation.
- A locally generated witness package is not independent reproduction.

## Donor intake

Third-party or upstream repositories may enter only as narrow adapters or fixtures. Every intake must record the upstream repository, immutable commit, license, copied paths, local changes, tests, and non-claims.

Wholesale source imports are prohibited. Adapters must be disabled by default and must not execute external skills, tools, transports, services, or physical actions without explicit authorization.

## Completion gate

Every runtime change must include:

1. deterministic input and output schema;
2. exact source and dependency pins;
3. positive and negative-path tests;
4. replay behavior;
5. receipt or artifact hashes;
6. failure-atomicity analysis;
7. explicit authority and production posture.

Default authority remains `EVIDENCE_ONLY`. Production readiness and independent replay remain unearned until separately demonstrated.
